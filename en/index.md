## Our position

SeatCraft (“the app”) creates seating charts and seating plans.
**The app handles your data only on your own device, and never sends any personal
information anywhere.**

## What the app stores

The app stores the following, entered by you, in a database on your device only.

- Rosters (participant names, readings, tags, attendance)
- Pairs to keep apart
- Seat layouts and the history of generated seating charts
- App settings, including your purchase state

This data:

- **Is never sent outside your device.** The app has no server.
- Cannot be seen by anyone else, including the developer.
- Is deleted from the device when you uninstall the app.

## Network use

Every feature of the app works offline. The only time the app itself talks to the
network is to ask the Google Play or App Store payment system to handle the in-app
purchase. No roster data or other data you entered is sent in the process.

## In-app purchase

The full version is purchased and restored through the Google Play / App Store payment
systems. Information handled as part of the payment is covered by the privacy policy of
the respective store. All the app receives back is whether the purchase was made.

## Analytics and advertising

The app performs no analytics, no behavioural tracking and no advertising, and contains
no SDK for any of them.

## Backups

A backup (JSON export) is created on your device and saved or moved by you, through the
sharing feature of your operating system. Data handling at the destination you choose
(cloud storage and so on) is covered by that service. The app never sends a backup
anywhere on its own.

## PDF export and sharing

Seating chart PDFs are created on your device. Printing and sharing happen through your
operating system's sharing feature, by your own action, and data handling at the
destination is covered by that service.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy is revised, we will announce it in the app or on the store page.
